Team Coaching

Group and Team High Performance Coaching

For senior leadership teams, executive groups and high-potential cohorts.

For senior teams, performance is rarely about capability alone. It is often shaped by trust, decision-making, accountability and the conversations people avoid.

I work with senior leadership teams, executive groups and high-potential cohorts to strengthen how they think and work together. My team coaching is grounded in real business challenges and pays close attention to the dynamics that shape how a team actually performs.

Who It Is For

This work is for senior teams where the usual team session is not enough.

I am most useful when the issue is not simply about roles, priorities or ways of working, but about what is happening underneath: the conversations people avoid, the patterns the group keeps repeating, and the gap between what the team says it wants and how it actually behaves.

Because I have worked at senior global level inside complex organisations, I understand the pressure, pace and politics of executive life. I bring that commercial understanding together with psychological and psychotherapy training, so the work can go deeper without becoming therapy.

  • Executive and senior leadership teams under real pressure to shift how they work
  • Newly formed or restructured teams where trust needs to build quickly
  • Teams leading transformation, growth or cultural change
  • Partner, director or functional leadership groups managing complex stakeholder demands
  • High-potential cohorts preparing for greater visibility and responsibility
  • Teams where tension, avoidance or slow decision-making is getting in the way
  • Groups with strong individuals who need to become a stronger collective

Teams need more than alignment on paper. They need enough trust to say what needs to be said, and enough discipline to follow through.

What the Work Focuses On

I work with the team as a whole, not just as a group of individuals. The coaching explores how the group communicates, makes decisions, handles pressure and holds itself accountable.

As work changes, teams need to be more deliberate about what remains human: judgement, trust, accountability and the quality of conversation.

  • Building enough trust for people to say what needs to be said
  • Getting clearer on what matters most and how the team will work together
  • Improving how the group makes decisions, challenges each other and follows through
  • Noticing the patterns, tensions or assumptions that may be getting in the way
  • Turning insight into practical changes in behaviour, rhythm and team routines

How the Team Coaching Works

The work starts with a confidential scoping conversation to understand the team’s context, business priorities and what needs to shift. I then shape the work around the team’s real challenges. This may include stakeholder input, diagnostic conversations, facilitated sessions and practical actions between sessions.

The work is psychologically informed, but firmly focused on team performance. It helps the group understand the patterns that shape how they communicate, make decisions, handle pressure and hold each other to account.

Where the work is organisation-sponsored, confidentiality and reporting are agreed at the start. Any feedback focuses on shared themes, progress and what the team has agreed to work on rather than private coaching content.
