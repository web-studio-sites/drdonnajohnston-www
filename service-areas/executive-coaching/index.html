Executive Coaching

Executive Coaching for Senior Leaders, Boards and Corporate Clients

For senior leaders seeking a confidential 1:1 coaching partnership.

I work with senior leaders, board-level executives and corporate clients who want space to think more clearly, steady themselves under pressure and lead with more confidence.

With more than 25 years’ experience in global people leadership and organisational change, I bring senior corporate experience together with psychological insight and a strong commercial lens. I have worked at global executive level in organisations where influence, judgement and steadiness under pressure really matter.

Clients often come to me at a point of shift: a bigger role, more visibility, a significant transition, or a need to look more honestly at how they lead when the pressure is high.

For Senior Leaders Operating at Scale

I work with senior executives, partners, directors, founders, emerging C-suite leaders and high-potential corporate talent who are operating in environments where judgement and influence really count. The work suits leaders whose impact depends as much on presence and relationships as on technical expertise.

  • Senior executives and board-level leaders
  • Partners, directors and functional leaders
  • Founders and entrepreneurial leaders
  • Emerging C-suite and high-potential leaders
  • Leaders stepping into larger, more visible or more complex roles
  • Leaders operating in high-pressure, high-scrutiny environments

Senior leadership now asks more of people than technical expertise. Leaders need to stay clear under pressure, understand their impact and bring others with them through complexity.

A Tailor-Made Coaching Experience

My coaching is confidential, thoughtful and appropriately challenging. I offer a calm space where leaders can step away from the pace of corporate life and look honestly at what is shaping their decisions, relationships and impact.

My approach draws on executive coaching, organisational psychology, psychotherapy training and senior corporate leadership experience. The work creates insight, but it always comes back to what leaders do differently in practice.

I do not work from a fixed coaching formula. I work with each client in a way that fits their context, paying attention to the patterns, assumptions and relationships that influence how they lead and make choices at senior level.

As work changes, leaders are being asked to exercise judgement, make sense of complexity and help people adapt. My coaching helps leaders stay grounded and effective through that change.

How 1:1 Executive Coaching Works

  1. 01

    1:1 executive coaching usually starts with a private chemistry conversation to understand the leader’s context and what they want to change or strengthen. From there, the work is shaped around clear outcomes, confidentiality and a practical way of working together.

  2. 02

    Each session focuses on the leader’s real work and live challenges. This might include preparing for important conversations, building confidence, navigating stakeholders, stepping into a bigger role or staying steady under pressure.

  3. 03

    I work with the visible leadership issue and the patterns that may be sitting behind it. Clients are supported to pause, reflect, test assumptions and try different ways of responding, so insight becomes useful in the day-to-day reality of leadership.

  4. 04

    The coaching can be a short, focused piece of work around a specific challenge or transition, or a longer partnership for deeper development. Progress is reviewed regularly to make sure the work stays useful, relevant and aligned to the outcomes agreed at the start.

My work is psychologically informed, but it is not therapy. The focus remains on leadership, performance, professional development and impact within the client’s organisational context.

Where coaching is organisation-sponsored, confidentiality is clearly contracted from the outset, with any reporting focused on agreed themes and progress rather than private coaching content.

Private and Corporate Engagements

I work with both private executive clients and organisations commissioning coaching for senior leaders. Engagements may be arranged directly with individuals, through HR or talent teams, as part of leadership development programmes, or for succession and transition support.

What Makes the Work Distinctive

Clients can expect a coaching relationship that is confidential, thoughtful and tailored to them. I create space for senior leaders to step back, look honestly at what is shaping their impact, and work through the patterns or assumptions that may be helping them or getting in the way.

The work is reflective, but always practical. It helps clients understand what is happening in their thinking, relationships and leadership habits, and then translate that insight into different choices and behaviour day to day.

This is not simply a sounding board. I work well with leaders who are prepared to look honestly at themselves, be thoughtfully challenged, and make practical changes in how they lead day to day.
