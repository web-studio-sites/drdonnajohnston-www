Importance of Critical Thinking

Critical thinking is a required competency and skill for leaders in organisations where rapid change is constant. By being able to really critically think for yourself, understanding your own values, behaviours and aspirations, as well as your colleagues, teams and the broader organisational context, you will be able to unlock your own potential, be able to better self-manage, which in turn fosters an environment of trust, improves collaboration within, and between, teams and achieving results.

About Donna

My work is grounded in senior corporate experience, doctoral research, executive coaching and psychotherapy training. My background includes doctoral research in organisational psychology and behavioural change, MSc-level training in psychotherapy, an Advanced Diploma in Gestalt Psychotherapy, and professional accreditation through the CIPD and EMCC.

I am also trained in a range of coaching, psychometric and leadership assessment approaches, which I draw on selectively and only where useful for the client's context.

How I Work

My work encourages individuals to achieve positive and sustainable change using a strengths-based approach. Key to this is raising levels of self-awareness, challenging assumptions and increasing understanding of organisational context.

I believe that individuals have the internal resources to find their own solutions and I help clients understand that there are choices to be made which have a direct bearing on the results they desire.

Service Areas

Coaching for individuals, senior leaders and whole teams, shaped around the work you are actually doing.

Develop Leaders

I develop leaders through coaching, I offer bespoke leadership development that is blended and grounded in industry best practices. I work with other excellent coaches from around the world who are also seasoned professionals. I can offer a modular, experiential, and creative approach to any programme design which can also be adapted to any leadership challenge an organisation or individual may face.

Coaching Supervision

Whether you are a new or established coach I offer an eclectic approach to supervision in a safe and confidential space for you to learn more about you and to reflect on your coaching practice. Working on a one to one or small group basis I offer time for reflection, nurturing and development of the individual coach and their coaching practice. I work on a one to one or small group basis to help coaches work ethically and help them develop their skills.

Executive Coaching for Senior Leaders

Group and Team High Performance Coaching
