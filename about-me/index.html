About Me

Why Donna

I bring a combination of experience that is unusual in executive coaching. I have worked at senior global level inside complex organisations, and I also bring doctoral research, coaching expertise and psychotherapy training to my work.

That means I understand the real pressure senior leaders are under, while also being able to work with confidence, self-belief, relationships and the patterns that shape how people lead.

In a changing world, I help leaders stay grounded, exercise judgement and keep the human part of leadership at the centre.

Depth of Expertise

My work is grounded in senior corporate experience, doctoral research, executive coaching and psychotherapy training. My background includes doctoral research in organisational psychology and behavioural change, MSc-level training in psychotherapy, an Advanced Diploma in Gestalt Psychotherapy, and professional accreditation through the CIPD and EMCC.

I am also trained in a range of coaching, psychometric and leadership assessment approaches, which I draw on selectively and only where useful for the client's context.

This breadth allows me to work with both the person and the wider system around them, keeping the coaching reflective, practical and rooted in real leadership challenges.
