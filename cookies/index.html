This Cookie Policy explains how [Company] (“we”, “us”) uses cookies and similar technologies on this website. It should be read alongside our Privacy Policy.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website places on your device when you visit. They are widely used to make websites work, or work more efficiently, and to provide information to the site owner or to third parties.

Your choice

When you first visit our website, a banner asks whether you accept cookies. Nothing that sets cookies is loaded until you choose. If you click Reject, no analytics or third-party cookies are set.

You can change your choice at any time using the Cookie settings link at the bottom of every page.

Cookies we use

With your permission, we use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our website, so we can improve it. It sets these cookies:

  • _ga: distinguishes visitors. Expires after 2 years.
  • ga[ID]: keeps track of your visit. Expires after 2 years.

These are only set if you click Accept, and are removed if you later change your choice to Reject.

We do not use advertising cookies.

Third-party services

  • Google Fonts: the website’s typeface (Open Sans) is loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your device’s IP address but does not set cookies.
  • Web3Forms: messages sent through the contact form are delivered by this service. It does not set cookies.
  • unpkg: some icons are loaded from this content delivery service, which receives your device’s IP address but does not set cookies.

Managing cookies in your browser

You can also block or delete cookies through your browser settings. For help, visit aboutcookies.org.
